## First 7 days: Stabilization

| Task | Owner | Frequency | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily post quality check | Community Lead | Daily | <2 corrections per day |
| Helpdesk response audit | Helpdesk Lead | Daily | FRT <2 hours |
| Spam/fraud moderation sweep | Moderators | Daily | 100% scam takedown within 30 min |
| Reactivation outreach execution | CRM/Support | Daily | Contact coverage >80% list |
| Zone signal collection (hotspots/events/rain) | Zone Captains | Daily | 1 useful local update per zone/day |
| KPI review and adjustments | Community Lead | Daily | Actions logged for next day |

## Minimum governance rules to pin

1. Only admin incentive messages are official.
2. No one from OLA asks for money or OTP in group/channel.
3. Keep chat relevant to trips, schemes, payout support.
4. Abuse/spam/fake info triggers removal after warning policy.
5. Helpdesk time window clearly displayed.
