# Mumbai Zone Hotspot and Field Onboarding Plan

This guide helps field teams onboard drivers consistently and feed useful demand signals
back into the WhatsApp Channel and Community.

## 1) Zone segmentation (practical operating map)

Keep zone naming simple for drivers and ops teams.

### Western Zone

Primary clusters:
- Andheri East/West
- Jogeshwari
- Goregaon
- Malad
- Kandivali
- Borivali

High-probability pickup points:
- Andheri station east/west auto stands
- DN Nagar metro feeder points
- Goregaon station and film city approach roads
- Malad station and link road commercial pockets
- Borivali station west and highway approach

### Central and Harbour Zone

Primary clusters:
- Dadar
- Sion
- Kurla
- Ghatkopar
- Chembur
- Wadala

High-probability pickup points:
- Dadar station east/west exits
- Kurla station and BKC connector roads
- Ghatkopar metro-station feeder points
- Chembur naka and business corridors

### South Mumbai Zone

Primary clusters:
- CST/Fort
- Colaba
- Churchgate/Marine Lines
- Lower Parel

High-probability pickup points:
- CST terminal area
- Churchgate business district
- Lower Parel offices and malls

### Navi Mumbai/Extended Zone (optional by scale)

Primary clusters:
- Vashi
- Nerul
- Belapur
- Airoli

High-probability pickup points:
- Vashi station and market
- Belapur CBD offices
- Nerul station connectors

## 2) Daily field onboarding route

Goal: consistent acquisition, not one-time spike.

### Morning route (07:30-11:30)

Priority:
1. CNG stations with high auto density
2. Major station auto queues
3. School/office corridor start points

Field script:
- 20-30 sec pitch
- QR scan assistance
- Confirm join completed before leaving

### Midday route (12:30-15:30)

Priority:
1. Repair shops/garages
2. Meal break clusters
3. Stand associations

Objective:
- onboard inactive/part-time drivers who are not usually online at peak morning

### Evening route (17:00-20:30)

Priority:
1. Station exits
2. Office district edges
3. Event venue surroundings

Objective:
- push same-day demand and incentive urgency

## 3) Field team deployment model

Minimum deployment:
- 1 Field Lead
- 3 Zone Captains (Western, Central/Harbour, South/Navi)
- 1 floating support resource

Daily expected output per captain:
- 25-40 verified joins/day
- 10 meaningful conversations/day
- 3 local demand signals/day

## 4) QR and onboarding assets checklist

- Laminated QR cards (A5) for individual captains
- A3 posters for stands/CNG stations
- Short URL backup in case QR scan fails
- Safety card: "No OTP/UPI/Password sharing"

## 5) Driver onboarding qualification (must capture)

Capture at join (via sheet or simple form):
- Name
- Mobile number
- Driver ID (if available)
- Primary zone
- Current activity status (active, 7d inactive, 15d inactive, 30d inactive)
- Preferred language (Hindi, Marathi, English)

## 6) Conversion script by driver type

### Already active driver
"Daily scheme clarity and peak-area alerts se aapka planning easy ho jayega."

### 7-15 day inactive driver
"Comeback plan milega with low-start target and support."

### 30+ day inactive driver
"Aapke liye assisted restart plan and direct helpdesk support rahega."

## 7) End-of-day field reporting format

Per zone captain submit by 21:00:
- area covered
- total approached
- total joined (verified)
- reactivation leads identified
- top 3 demand insights
- issues/escalations

## 8) Weekly field optimization

Every Sunday, re-allocate effort based on:
- join conversion by location
- quality of local demand signals
- reactivation lift by captain-led clusters

Drop low-yield spots. Double down on top 20% locations by conversion.
