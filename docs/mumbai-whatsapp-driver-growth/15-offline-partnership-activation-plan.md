# Offline Partnership Activation Plan (Mumbai)

This plan drives channel/community growth through local ecosystem partners.

## 1) Goal

- Increase verified joins from trusted physical points.
- Improve reactivation by targeting inactive drivers at service locations.

## 2) Partner categories

Priority partners:
- CNG pumps with high auto traffic
- Auto stands and union points
- Local garages and spare-part shops
- Tea/snack points near stand clusters
- Finance/insurance assistance desks (where relevant)

## 3) Offer design for partners

Keep offer simple and compliant:
- "Official driver update channel QR placement"
- periodic thank-you recognition for high conversion partners
- optional co-branded awareness material

Do not create cash-heavy referral structures without policy approval.

## 4) Activation kit per partner

Minimum kit:
- 1 A3 QR poster
- 2 A5 counter cards
- 1 anti-fraud mini card
- partner briefing script (2 minutes)
- weekly performance sheet

## 5) Partner onboarding process

1. Identify high-footfall location.
2. Meet owner/manager and explain official purpose.
3. Place QR visibility assets at entry and billing points.
4. Train one staff member to prompt join during idle time.
5. Capture partner in tracker with zone tag.

## 6) Field conversion script at partner point

```
Official OLA Mumbai update channel join karo.
Daily scheme clarity, best area-time alerts, aur support updates milenge.
Fake messages avoid karne ke liye sirf official channel follow karo.
```

## 7) Weekly partner performance tracking

Track:
- footfall estimate
- scans
- verified joins
- retained after 7 days
- reactivated leads identified

Promote top 20% partners and revisit bottom 20% with better placement.

## 8) Zone-wise rollout suggestion (first month)

Week 1:
- 5 CNG points
- 3 major stands

Week 2:
- add 4 garages
- add 3 station-side tea points

Week 3:
- expand to Navi/extended zones if conversion is stable

Week 4:
- prune low-yield locations and scale top performers

## 9) Risk controls

- No unverified third-party messaging in partner premises.
- Refresh QR every month if misuse found.
- Remove materials from non-compliant partners.
- Record every issue in escalation log.

## 10) Partner review rhythm

Weekly 20-minute review:
- top conversion locations
- issue locations
- asset replacement needs
- next-week deployment plan
